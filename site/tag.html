GPS quality: time to fix and HDOP

Time to fix (TTF) is how long the GPS searched before reporting a position; lower saves battery. HDOP is the horizontal dilution of precision: lower means the satellites were better spread and the position more precise (0–1 excellent, 2–4 good, 5–9 moderate, 10+ poor; 99 means no fix). TTF figures use only messages that got a fix.
