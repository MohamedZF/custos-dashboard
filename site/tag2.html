Power and health

Supply voltage

The supercap as the tag's PMIC reads it (about 30–60 mV under the cap). Health frames give it to 10 mV with the hour's lowest; position and start-up frames to 100 mV.

Temperature

The nRF chip's temperature from each health frame, in whole degrees (0 means 0 °C or colder).

Self-tests and events

Self-tests from the newest health or position frame. Events are what the tag logged in the hour before each health frame.

Activity

Activity score per 15-minute cycle

Median of each cycle's accelerometer readings, 0 (still) to 7, from the activity frames. Each frame covers the hour's four cycles.

Posture and mortality

Pitch range per cycle (head up is positive), orientation changes per hour, and the mortality check's latest state.

GPS

Positions

GPS fixes from position and start-up frames. The newest is the large marker.

GPS results

What each position frame's search found. Indoors the tag hears no satellites and backs off to one search an hour.

RSSI of each frame as the backend reported it. Below about −135 dBm frames start to go missing.

Delivery

From Sigfox sequence numbers: every frame the tag sends gets the next number, so a gap is a frame that never arrived.
